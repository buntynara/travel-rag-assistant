# ABC Travel Guide

ABC is a new mountain-and-lake tourist city located near the Maharashtra–Karnataka border. The city was officially established as a planned tourism destination in **August 2026** with the vision of promoting eco-tourism, sustainable development, and smart travel infrastructure. Although recently developed, ABC has quickly become a preferred weekend getaway for families, couples, solo travelers, photographers, and adventure enthusiasts due to its scenic landscapes, clean environment, and modern facilities.

Situated at an elevation of approximately **1,450 meters (4,760 feet)** above sea level, ABC enjoys a pleasant climate throughout the year. The city is surrounded by lush green forests, rolling hills, crystal-clear lakes, waterfalls, and nature trails. The average daytime temperature ranges between **12°C and 28°C**, making it suitable for outdoor sightseeing in every season.

ABC has been designed as a **vehicle-friendly smart tourism city** featuring electric shuttle buses, dedicated cycling tracks, pedestrian-only heritage zones, free public Wi-Fi in major tourist areas, and AI-powered tourist information kiosks. The city also follows strict environmental policies, including zero single-use plastics in tourist zones, rainwater harvesting, and renewable energy for public infrastructure.

The local economy is driven primarily by tourism, hospitality, handicrafts, organic farming, and cultural events. Visitors can explore scenic viewpoints, heritage-inspired architecture, botanical gardens, museums, adventure parks, and lakeside promenades while experiencing a unique blend of traditional culture and modern urban planning.

ABC is particularly popular for its annual **Festival of Lantern Clouds**, **Crystal Harvest Fair**, lakeside music festivals, night markets, and international food carnival, attracting thousands of domestic and international visitors each year.

The city is well connected by **ABC International Airport**, express highways, and high-speed rail services from major Indian cities. Local transportation includes electric buses, trams, app-based taxis, bicycle rentals, and walking trails, allowing tourists to travel conveniently between attractions.

ABC is considered one of India's safest tourist destinations, with dedicated tourist police, multilingual information centers, 24×7 emergency response services, and digital visitor assistance available throughout the city.
