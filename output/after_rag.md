**5-Day Travel Plan: Ahmedabad to ABC**
=====================================

### Introduction

This 5-day travel plan from Ahmedabad to ABC includes flights, hotels, and sightseeing activities, all within a budget of ₹1 Lakh. The plan is designed to provide a comfortable and enjoyable trip to ABC, a new mountain-and-lake tourist city located near the Maharashtra-Karnataka border.

### Flight Information

* **Departure from Ahmedabad:** September 11
* **Flight Options:**
        + SkyNova Airways SN101: Departure at 07:15, Arrival at 09:05 (approximate fare: ₹4,500-7,000)
        + Blue Horizon Air BH221: Departure at 11:40, Arrival at 13:35 (approximate fare: ₹4,500-7,000)
        + Crystal Wings CW309: Departure at 16:10, Arrival at 18:00 (approximate fare: ₹4,500-7,000)
        + Zenith Air ZA455: Departure at 20:00, Arrival at 21:50 (approximate fare: ₹4,500-7,000)
* **Return Flight:** Take a return flight from ABC to Ahmedabad on September 16 (approximate fare: ₹4,500-7,000)

### Hotel Options

* **Budget:**
        + Lakeview Inn: ₹2,200/night (free Wi-Fi, breakfast included, 2 km from city center)
        + Maple Nest Hotel: ₹2,800/night (family rooms, airport pickup)
* **Mid-Range:**
        + Crystal Crown Residency: ₹5,800/night (rooftop restaurant, swimming pool)
        + Azure Echo Suites: ₹6,500/night (lake view, gym, complimentary breakfast)
* **Luxury:** Options available, but not included in this budget breakdown

### Itinerary

* **Day 1 (September 11): Ahmedabad to ABC**
        + Flight: Take the SkyNova Airways SN101 from Ahmedabad to ABC
        + Hotel: Check-in at Lakeview Inn or Maple Nest Hotel
        + Sightseeing: Visit the Crystal Crown Citadel, an ancient hilltop fortress with guided tours and a sunrise viewpoint
* **Day 2: ABC**
        + Sightseeing: Explore the Azure Echo Lake, where you can enjoy boating, a musical fountain, and a family picnic area
        + Local Transportation: Use the Electric City Shuttle or Taxi to travel around the city
        + Food: Try the famous Moonleaf Stew, Golden Ember Bread, or Crystal Berry Tart
* **Day 3: ABC**
        + Sightseeing: Visit the Lumina Sky Bridge, a glass-bottom bridge with night illumination
        + Adventure: Explore the Whispering Bamboo Valley, with nature trails and bird watching
        + Shopping: Visit the Prism Avenue, known for crystal décor and designer boutiques
* **Day 4: ABC**
        + Sightseeing: Visit the Starfall Observatory, with astronomy sessions and interactive science exhibits (book tickets in advance)
        + Local Transportation: Use the Skyline Tram or Bicycle Rentals to travel around the city
        + Food: Try the River Mint Soup, Firegrain Pilaf, or Sapphire Spice Tea
* **Day 5: ABC to Ahmedabad**
        + Flight: Take a return flight from ABC to Ahmedabad
        + Hotel: Check-out from the hotel and depart from ABC

### Budget Breakdown

* **Flights:** ₹9,000-14,000 (approximate)
* **Hotel (4 nights):** ₹8,800-11,200 (approximate)
* **Sightseeing, food, and local transportation:** ₹10,000-15,000 (approximate)
* **Total:** ₹27,800-40,200 (approximate)

This 5-day travel plan should fit within the budget of ₹1 Lakh, depending on the specific flight and hotel options chosen.
